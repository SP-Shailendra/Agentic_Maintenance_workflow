# Synthetic demo content, not an OEM document

## WD-101 | Bearing wear
Symptoms: rising vibration RMS with a gradual bearing-housing temperature increase.
Recommended tasks: isolate and lock out the motor, inspect bearing clearance, replace the bearing, and verify alignment.
Parts: BRG-101 bearing assembly, GRS-201 high-speed grease.
Required skills: VIB, MECH.

## WD-102 | Cooling fan degradation
Symptoms: motor temperature rises while airflow and fan speed fall.
Recommended tasks: inspect fan guards, verify motor current, and replace the fan assembly if damaged.
Parts: FAN-301 cooling fan assembly.
Required skills: MECH, ELEC.
