## Reservation Timing

The source design notes are inconsistent about whether parts may be reserved while preparing an approval package. This implementation follows the controlling safety rule: availability checks are read-only, and inventory reservations are committed only after an explicit human approval. Rejection therefore leaves stock unchanged.

## Evaluation Boundary

Any detection measurements generated by this repository are synthetic-demo results. They are not OEM guidance, clinical-operating instructions, customer claims, or evidence of real-world plant performance. The included manuals are labeled synthetic and must not be used as real service instructions.
